How it works

Women helping women — at every stage.

Fittfi is not a course, a newsletter, or an app. It's a community where women who have figured something out pass it on to the women who haven't yet.

Regenerative by design

Today's student becomes tomorrow's mentor. Every woman who levels up brings the next one with her. The community sustains itself — and grows stronger with every member who moves forward.

→Henny helped Maya spot a CHF 2,500 annual fee she was overpaying
→Henny helped Ruby open her first investment account after years of believing markets weren't for her
→Maya is now mentoring Lee on how to build a dashboard and read her PNL
→Lee will help the next woman take her first step

Community delivers the human connection. AI handles the matching. Together they make personalised financial guidance scalable.
